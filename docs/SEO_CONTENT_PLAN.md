## Catalog editorial queue

Public catalog search excerpts show short descriptions for Baby hamper, Bag hamper, Chocolate hamper, Chocolate tower and Dress Hamper (including a spelling error). Verify their current admin records and actual photographs before editing. Suggested description structure: what the design is, occasion, confirmed contents/materials, personalisation options, starting-price inclusions, enquiry next step. Never invent contents, price, reviews or delivery commitments. Correct visible spelling without unnecessarily changing URLs.

## Content maintenance

Use the admin journal for ongoing publishing. The two bundled guides are code-owned fallbacks until an admin creates a row with the matching slug. Draft or publish that row to control the public page. Existing database content has precedence and is not overwritten by deploy. Confirm the articles' factual descriptions and actual publication date before releasing. Replace the general brand image with a relevant approved original photo when available.

For future posts, prefer real order case studies with customer consent and photographs, specific decisions (occasion, style, budget where approved), and a relevant catalog link. Avoid thin locality variants, fake comparisons or fabricated customer quotes.
